\section*{About this volume}
This volume contains the complete pronunciation practice key followed by the keys to Units 1–18. Answers follow the same unit order as the Learner’s Book and retain the exercise and item labels used there.\par
Models, required targets, defensible alternatives and explanations remain separate where the original keys distinguish them. A valid alternative need not match a model word for word; a stated exercise target still governs the task.\par
The contents gives the starting page of each key. Section numbers remain local to their unit. References to Cook’s printed pages and scans retain their source locators.\par
