## Findings and resolution

The one material finding, **SH-CK-REV-M01**, concerned missing stressed-root scope in the initial descriptions of fronting, breaking and the two other diphthongization processes. The final text supplies local qualifiers, a precise breaking-table heading and the additional source locator in note 2. I reread the changed passages.

This correction does not make every process exclusive to stressed syllables. Fulk explicitly allows brightening and front mutation in unstressed syllables (Appendix A IV.26/30, printed p.111 / PDF123). The present examples are scoped to stressed roots; breaking itself is explicitly excluded from unstressed syllables (V.45, printed p.113 / PDF125). The final wording preserves that distinction.

The other reviewer’s two refinements are also verified: the first occurrence of *light syllable* points to §7, and the **dēman** explanation refers to loss of the conditioning sound while preserving its surviving **-an**. No material or optional finding remains open in this review.
