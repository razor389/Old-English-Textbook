# Sound-history appendix

[Why Old English forms vary: a short sound history](student.md) explains breaking, umlaut, ablaut, palatal changes, vowel loss, contraction and analogy. It is optional reading for linguistic interest. Definitions and supplied meanings make it usable without completing the course; familiarity with noun and verb paradigms makes the comparisons more useful. It adds no exercises or recall requirements.

The [PDF and reproduction notes](../../typesetting/sound-history/README.md) use the shared course layout. The existing [pronunciation guide](../../guides/pronunciation/student.md) remains the practical introduction to reading aloud.

The [contract](coverage.json) was saved before prose at `86ca81e`. Independent [Cook](../../sources/audits/sound-history/cook/report.md), [Fulk](../../sources/audits/sound-history/fulk/report.md) and [course-design](../../curriculum/proposals/sound-history/report.md) audits establish the evidence and scope. The [coordinator reconciliation](../../sources/audits/sound-history/coordinator/reconciliation.json), [example specification](examples.json), [author notes](author-notes.json) and [amendments](authoring-amendments.json) distinguish original prose, exact source forms, declared spelling conventions and reconstructed earlier forms.

All 44 Old English example forms lead to 23 existing dictionary headwords. The [additive access inventory](glossary-access.json) records every actual example and distinguishes words from letters, endings, four reconstructions and one Gothic cognate. The source variants **hefon**, **hēafodes** and **hēh** have explicit pointers in the prose to **heofon**, **hēafod** and **hēah**. No new dictionary headword, lexical sense or exercise-access entry is needed.

The independent [Cook review](review/cook.md) and [Fulk review](review/fulk.md) cover the actual learner text. The [preservation boundary](course-boundary.json) protects 191 earlier course and glossary artifacts, including the cumulative registers and accepted PDFs. Appendix inclusion does not change the eighteen-unit teaching sequence or the assignment of Cook's exercises.
