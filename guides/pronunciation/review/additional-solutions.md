Proposed task ID: **PRON-P04c**. Proposed learner item: **D3. heofonan**, “heaven”. The standing directions ask for the number of syllables, the stressed syllable, and any doubled consonant and its pronunciation.

Independent answer: **heofonan** has **three syllables**, **heo-fo-nan**, with the main stress on **heo-**. **eo** is a single short diphthong and does not make two syllables. There is **no doubled consonant**. The two occurrences of **n** are separated by a vowel and do not form **nn**. For the accompanying reading, **f** has the voiced sound of *van* because it stands between voiced vowel sounds.

No material ambiguity affects this answer. The syllable divisions identify the three vowel nuclei and stress; the task does not require a narrow phonetic transcription or an analysis of consonant syllabification.

## Independent assessment of the adapted sentence

Proposed learner sentence: **God gescēop heofonan and eorðan.**

Independent translation: **God created heaven and earth.**

This is a grammatical finite declarative clause. **God** is its subject; **gescēop** supplies the singular past-tense predicate; **heofonan and eorðan** are its coordinated objects. The adaptation removes the introductory prepositional phrase **On anginne** from Cook's sentence and places the subject before the verb. The predicate, objects, conjunction, and their inflected forms retain their original roles. The subject–verb–object order is ordinary and does not require a new construction or an implied missing constituent.

I inspected Cook, selected second edition (1895), Reader I, printed p.123 / PDF139, first prose line, in `sources/audits/pronunciation/cook/cook-p123-pdf139.png`. Its sentence is **On anginne gescēop God heofonan and eorðan.** Cook's own note 2 observes that the source's verb-before-subject order is probably influenced by Latin. The revised sentence must be labelled an **adaptation of this specific sentence**, with the deletion and reordering recorded; it must not be labelled an exact quotation or merely a spelling normalization.

The revised sentence still supports all three existing E tasks: the two syllables and consonants of **gescēop**, voiced **f** in **heofonan**, and voiced **ð** after **r** in **eorðan**. It removes the unresolved pronunciation of the optional noun **anginn** without removing any required sound category from this practice set.
