## Concerns recorded before key access

1. **Word-final doubled consonants:** the learner master requires prolonged final **l** in **full** and makes it a task target. Cook §11 explicitly distinguishes final from medial doubled consonants, and Fulk’s sound table writes **ful(l)**. The source basis for requiring a final-duration contrast therefore needs review. The solution above records the guide’s demand without certifying it.
2. **anginne:** the supplied hard **g** must be verified for this particular prefixed form. Fulk’s palatal bare **ġinnan** and qualified **ng** rules prevent a safe inference from adjacent letters alone. This issue does not change the independently recoverable answer of three syllables, middle stress, and medial long **nn**.
3. **No other task ambiguity:** the 25 requested answers can be recovered from the learner material without memorizing IPA or independently supplying an untaught word meaning. Oral reading quality cannot be verified from a written solution record.
