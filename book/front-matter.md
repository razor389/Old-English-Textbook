# Old English

A grammar-led course

## Learner volume

This volume contains the pronunciation guide, eighteen grammar units, the optional sound-history appendix, and both reference glossaries. Answers to the exercises are collected in the separate Answer Key.

The table of contents lists the units and their main sections. The grammatical index points to explanations and paradigms, including explicitly limited recognition material. Its references do not extend the scope stated in a lesson.

Section numbers within a unit remain local to that unit. Cook’s exercise and item numbers are retained. Contents and index page numbers refer to this volume; references to printed source pages and scans retain their original locators.

The course uses Albert S. Cook’s Exercises in Old English and the second edition of A First Book in Old English (1895). Its practical organization follows the grammar-led model of Lambdin’s Gothic course. Source notes identify the evidence for individual readings, forms and explanations.

## Key volume

This volume contains the complete pronunciation practice key followed by the keys to Units 1–18. Answers follow the same unit order as the Learner’s Book and retain the exercise and item labels used there.

Models, required targets, defensible alternatives and explanations remain separate where the original keys distinguish them. A valid alternative need not match a model word for word; a stated exercise target still governs the task.

The contents gives the starting page of each key. Section numbers remain local to their unit. References to Cook’s printed pages and scans retain their source locators.
