# Complete learner book: page review

Accepted for the final 967-page learner PDF, with one nonblocking contents page-break note. No material layout, assembly, folio or visible glyph finding remains.

Reviewer: `/root/sound_fulk`; Beads issue `oec-book-review`. The reviewer did not author the assembly, front matter, index typesetting or raster verifier. Earlier roles included Fulk appendix evidence/review, the late-unit semantic index proposal and the independent early-index review. ## Actual inspection

- All **967 pages** inspected in **61 labelled contact sheets** as reduced structural overviews.
- All **967 footer strips** inspected at native pixel resolution in **41 labelled folio sheets**; every printed folio agrees with its caption/PDF label.
- All **37 new/blank pages** individually inspected at native page resolution: front matter 1-12, index 951-967, and blank versos 22, 44, 96, 118, 210, 240, 462 and 560.
- All **22 component openings** individually inspected as native raw page rasters, plus seven detail pages: 14, 16, 67, 783, 786, 788 and 794. These show pronunciation glyphs and IPA, adjective tables, appendix rules/quantities and dense glossary entries.
- All **930 retained learner pages** have exactly equal decoded RGB header/body pixels in their accepted standalone source and assembled book. There are no mismatches or cutoff-guard flags.

This records **168 actual image views**, including all **139 required learner proof artifacts** and 29 additional native pages. The JSON receipt embeds each proof path, SHA-256, covered page IDs and actual observation, and maps every physical page to its contact and native footer proofs. All 66 pages inspected individually at native resolution are explicitly identified.

The reduced contact pages establish layout, sequence, margins, running-head continuity, table preservation and the absence of unexpected blank or clipped content. They do not support a claim that all 967 pages were read word by word at full resolution. Exact retained-body preservation carries forward the prior detailed acceptance of source text, tables and special characters. New pages and changed footers were inspected natively. Click navigation and semantic index verification have separate receipts.

## New pages, characters and folios

The title, volume explanation and contents are clear. Contents leaders and page-number columns remain aligned even for long titles. All 17 grammatical-index pages have readable headings, subentries, recognition/supplied qualifications and cross-references; wrapped entries fit their columns. No table clipping, text overlap, footer collision or unexpected blank page was found. Native checks show intact ash, thorn, eth, hooked vowels, macrons, IPA signs, arrows and reconstruction marks.

Roman labels run i-xii; the title's i is unprinted, and ii-xii are printed. Arabic labels continue from 1 through 955. The eight inserted blank versos occupy folios 10, 32, 84, 106, 198, 228, 450 and 548; each is entirely empty and has no printed folio. All component starts are recto. Printed numbers sit at the expected outside edge with safe clearance.
