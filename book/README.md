# Complete book assembly

The [Learner’s Book](../typesetting/full-book/book.pdf) and [Answer Key](../typesetting/full-book/answer-key.pdf) bring together the accepted course exports. The learner volume contains the pronunciation guide, Units 1–18, the sound-history appendix and both glossaries. The key contains pronunciation answers and the eighteen unit keys.

The [assembly contract](coverage.json), saved at `feb01c8` before implementation, adds front matter, contents, continuous pagination, PDF navigation and a grammatical-topic index. It adds no teaching, exercise, answer or lexical entry. All 221 files in the [preservation boundary](preservation.json) retain their accepted bytes.

The [contents manifest](contents.json) fixes the 22 learner and 19 key components and all 123 Markdown/TeX/PDF source identities. Each component retains its original local section and exercise numbers, source locators, content, font settings and logical paradigms. Each begins on a recto. Arabic pagination is continuous within each volume after Roman front matter.

The [grammatical index](grammar-index.json) has 447 entries under 112 substantive headings, 74 see headings and 32 see-also groups. Its 360 targets point to actual explanations and paradigms; 493 distinct page references remain after coalescing same-page targets within an entry. Final pages are resolved from the compiled PDF, not estimated from source page numbers. Reference and supplied scopes remain those of the lessons.

Independent [early-half](reviews/index-early.md) and [late-half](reviews/index-late.md) reviews checked the learner text and source targets. [Amendments](amendments.json) retain the terminology correction, more precise locators and one evidence-line correction; the original proposals remain unchanged. The contract and proposal files preserve their pre-build stage states. Final export acceptance is recorded separately in the [export review](../typesetting/full-book/export-review.json).

The [front-matter master](front-matter.md) is the only new prose outside the index. The [typesetting notes](../typesetting/full-book/README.md) give reproduction commands and the complete source, text, navigation, image and independent visual-review evidence.
