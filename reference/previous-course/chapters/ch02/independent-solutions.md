## Neuter-rule revision: independent response

After the user requested an explanation of neuter **-u** versus zero, the reviewer read the revised student §1 and revised review 3 before consulting the revised key. The unchanged twenty tasks retain the responses above. The added portion of `ch02-review-03` is answerable as follows:

**sǣd** is a monosyllabic strong neuter with a long vowel **ǣ**, hence a heavy stem under the rule in §1. Such nouns normally take no nominative plural ending. The noun therefore stays **sǣd**; **ðæt/ðā** distinguishes the singular and plural phrases.

The supplied **hof/hofu** and **word/word** contrasts illustrate the rule without being requested for independent recall. No new noun-production vocabulary is required by the revised task.
