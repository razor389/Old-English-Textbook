## Reading

The printed learner clause is:

> Sēo eorðe sōðlice wæs īdel and ǣmtig;

| ID | Independent answer | Dependency check |
|---|---|---|
| ch01-reading-01 | The earth is being described; it was empty or void. | `eorðe` is taught; the past linking verb and both descriptive words are supplied. |
| ch01-reading-02 | The article is **sēo** and the noun **eorðe**. Both are feminine, singular, nominative. **and** joins **īdel** and **ǣmtig**, the two predicate adjectives. | The article/noun analysis is taught; the support explicitly explains the adjective construction and coordination. |
| ch01-reading-03 | **Now the earth was empty and void.** A close explanatory rendering is “The earth, for its part, was empty and empty/void.” | The translation retains the past tense and both supplied adjective meanings. `sōðlice` is a connective, not a command to use present tense. |

The extracted clause can end with an English period in a translation even though its source text ends with a semicolon. The learner explicitly knows that the source sentence continues. A defensible alternate rendering from the provided support is “The earth, for its part, was void and empty.” Choosing the natural pair “empty and void” need not invent an independent sense such as “without shape.”
