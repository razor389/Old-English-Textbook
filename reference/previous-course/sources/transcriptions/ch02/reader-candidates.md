### Proposed learner presentation, separate from the transcription

> and ðā fuglas bēon gemanigfielde ofer eorðan.

Changes: remove apparatus cue **²¹** and the closing quotation mark whose opening lies outside the extract. Preserve the final period, lowercase **and**, every word, and every vowel mark. Record these changes explicitly; they are apparatus and quotation-boundary omissions, with no lexical or grammatical rewriting. The learner origin category can be **declared normalization** for these display changes.

The phrase **ðā fuglas** supplies the intended plural recognition target. The verb and participial construction and the phrase **ofer eorðan** need supplied support; Chapter 1's nominative **eorðe** does not establish knowledge of **eorðan**. The scan does not resolve an accusative/dative label merely from this syncretic spelling. Do not ask for independent case identification without further teaching and evidence.

## Deferred alternative: `ch02-reader-darkness`

- Original identifier: Reader I, the coordinated darkness clause immediately after the earth clause assigned in Chapter 1.
- Printed page: **123**; selected PDF page: **139**.
- Locator: second and third prose lines after the introductory editorial paragraph. These are **editorially counted lines**, not printed line numbers.
- Image: [printed p.123, detail](../ch01/first-book-p123-detail.png).
- Source origin category: **exact source**.
- Proposed crosswalk disposition: **deferred**: its full prepositional phrase contains both a genitive modifier and the additional noun **brādnesse**, exceeding the initially proposed four-form support. It also has no article before the plural subject.

> and ðīestru⁷ wǣron⁶ ofer⁸ ðǣre⁴ nēowolnesse⁹ brādnesse¹⁰;

The source contains **brādnesse** and contains no **ðā** before **ðīestru**. Omitting the former or inserting the latter would change the language, not merely remove apparatus. Note **4**, not 3, follows **ðǣre**.

The relevant source notes read:

- Note **4**: **See se.**
- Note **6**: **See wesan.**
- Note **7**: **Plural, like Lat. tenebrae.**
- Note **8**: **Governs brādnesse.**
- Note **9**: **Genitive, dependent on brādnesse (153. i).**
- Note **10**: **See 166.**

The phrase is not simply **ofer ðǣre nēowolnesse**. Cook explicitly identifies **nēowolnesse** as a genitive dependent on **brādnesse**. The syncretic article form **ðǣre** should not receive an unsupported case label in an abbreviated gloss.
