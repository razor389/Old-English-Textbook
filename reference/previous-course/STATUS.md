## Editorial decisions established

- No prior Old English is assumed. Readers know standard grammar terminology; a terminology appendix remains an option.
- The user requests the economy of Thackston or Lambdin: terse rules and paradigms, minimal coaching, and a compact key with shared explanations. The revision retains all thirty nouns, twenty-five tasks, three examples, and four supplied reading forms.
- Lessons map one-to-one to Cook's numbered exercises, in source order, with every item included. Grammar and vocabulary are determined by the complete exercise; reader selections remain adjustable. Chapters 1–2 include all of Exercises 1–2.
- Roughly thirty new vocabulary items per lesson is acceptable; grammar scope is controlled separately.
- Evidence-backed corrections to Cook are authorized, with the original retained and the change documented.
- Chapter 1 correction C01 changes Cook's **wǣstm** to short-vowel **wæstm**, supported by Baker's glossary and Stenroos (2024), p.118, Table 1. Valid gender variants are not treated as errors.
- Source references and editorial commentary belong in section endnotes, with ordinary task numbering in the lesson. Internal IDs and detailed evidence remain in the source comments and coverage record.
- Markdown remains the editable lesson format. LuaLaTeX supplies each complete chapter and separate complete key.
