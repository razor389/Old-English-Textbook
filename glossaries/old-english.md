# Old English–English glossary

This glossary covers the Old English in all eighteen units and their keys, including tables, readings, alternatives and source notes, and in the pronunciation guide and its key. It is a reference, not a new memorization list. The exercise instructions still determine what you must know and what is supplied.

Look under the dictionary headword, normally the infinitive for a verb. Strong verbs give the preterite first singular, preterite plural and past participle; weak verbs give the preterite and participle. Distinctive presents and irregular verbs have their forms labelled. Selected difficult forms, including vowel-changing third-person singulars, have **see** entries. Routine inflections belong to the unit paradigms.

Nouns give gender, genitive singular and nominative plural where established. Adjectives and adverbs give selected comparison forms or useful stem clues. Words appearing only in a source note may have a limited citation rather than a complete set of principal parts. A missing cell supplies no form by implication. Meanings and construction notes retain the limits of the cited use.

The order is a, æ, b … t, þ/ð, u … z. Length marks and hooks do not affect alphabetization; they remain essential parts of the printed spelling. Thorn and eth share a sorting position. Words beginning ge- are under g. Ordinary forms are listed in lowercase; proper-name capitalization is retained. Sentence-initial capitals do not create separate entries.

Abbreviations: n. noun; v. verb; adj. adjective; adv. adverb; prep. preposition; conj. conjunction; pron. pronoun; interj. interjection; m., f., n. masculine, feminine, neuter; gen. genitive; nom. nominative; pres. present; pret. preterite; ptcp. participle; sg. singular; pl. plural; comp. comparative; superl. superlative. Numbers 1 and 3 identify persons. Parenthesized (ge) preserves the source's optional prefix notation; it does not license adding ge- to other words.

Case and construction notes qualify the meaning beside them. A supplied expression is not a general word-for-word translation rule. Source spellings corrected in the course, deliberately wrong examples and reconstructed forms are identified separately at the end.

The definitions and spelling follow the course's checked Cook sources, with the recorded Fulk, Wright and dictionary support where used. Principal parts include checked reference formations from the stated grammatical rules as well as exact source citations. This glossary is newly arranged reference material. The companion English–Old English glossary indexes exercise meanings and constructions.

## Quoted errors and reconstructions

These notices preserve what the book quotes. A spelling rejected in a particular exercise is not thereby declared impossible in every historical context.

**āgyltan**. Weak verb: sin; fail in duty. In Cook 34.9 this is an erroneous hint for reward; see āgyldan (strong III), whose contracted third singular is āgylt. [C06].

**dægas**. Deliberate learner error; requested plural is dagas.

**fiscnað**. Unselected respelling of the source word; the course retains fiscnoð.

**fōnende**. Rejected mechanical present participle; selected form is fōnde.

**hwælas**. Deliberate learner error; requested plural is hwalas.

**mynsteru**. Unselected answer in the course exercise; see the selected plural under mynster. This does not rule out every historical use of the spelling.

**sǣwa**. Reconstructed genitive plural of sǣ, marked with an asterisk in the book; it is not tested as an attested form.

**swētrra**. Rejected unsimplified comparative genitive plural; selected form is swētra.

**unnað**. Rejected manufactured indicative plural; selected present plural is unnon.

**wastmas**. Deliberate learner error; requested plural is wæstmas.

**wǣstm**. Cook’s erroneous quantity mark in the cited hint; see wæstm, with short æ. [C01].

**wīsrra**. Rejected unsimplified comparative genitive plural; selected form is wīsra.

**wundas**. Rejected masculine-pattern plural of the feminine wund in this context; selected plural is wunda.
