# Coordinator reading-witness reconciliation

The initial source-first witness is unchanged. It was prepared from the three source images before opening the other current translations. It is an author/coordinator witness, not the later independent learner review.

Comparison with the exact checked text supports these refinements for the eventual key:

- Duties: **wlanc** in this argument is the wealthy person, though its wider lexical range includes proud. Preserve the contrast between worldly life and eternal life, the gift/distribution, and the hundredfold repayment. The assigned argument ends at **heofonum.**; the surrounding speech continues beyond this extract.
- Preface: **Bōclæden** can be rendered book-Latin or literary Latin; the task is not a claim that Alfred translated every Latin book. **for mistlicum ... bisgum** locates his work amid the varied and manifold cares; the practical obstruction of his kingship must remain clear. The text explains his alternation between literal and sense translation.
- Prayer: **āworhte** concerns what the speaker has deserved or incurred by his conduct in the forgiving petition. The initial witness's English note should not be treated as an exact transcription: the source's infinitive is **wyrceanne**. **gehīersumnesse** is obedience, not intercession; **gestaðela**, **Sīe**, and the distinction between personal **ðē** and relative **ðe** follow the checked source.

These are translation and transcription reconciliations, not assertions of errors in Cook. Final key prose and all learner tasks still require a fresh independent solve and comparison.
