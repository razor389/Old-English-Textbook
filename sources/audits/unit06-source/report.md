# Unit 6 independent source audit

The selected reading is **FB-XII-APOLLONIUS-FISHERMAN-GIFT-01**, “The fisherman’s gift and first direction (excerpt)”: Cook’s 1895 second edition, Reader XII, printed p167 / PDF183, editorial prose lines3–10. It contains76 whitespace words. The selection begins at **Ðā, sōna swā** and ends at the period after **ceastre.**, partway through printed line10. It covers the fisherman’s care, gift, and first complete spoken instruction. It is not the whole encounter or the whole speech.

The exact line transcription, joined source text, learner excerpt, source notes and presentation changes are separate files. The learner excerpt alone adds a closing quotation at the selected endpoint. A retained600dpi source image confirms **læg**, without a length mark, and **gīet**, with long ī. The prefixed **ūpāhōf** belongs to ūp-ā-hębban; it must not be credited as an already learned unprefixed hębban form. Cook’s own note reports the manuscript’s fæstnesse beside his printed ārfæstnesse; this is preserved as source editorial information, not silently treated as our correction.

`audit.json` records every token,60 distinct form/sense requirements,10 visible support groups, and the construction boundaries. `prior-inputs.json` retains actual earlier register records and the input hashes. Earlier counts are242 lemmas,3198 forms,265 senses and205 grammatical features. Earlier supplied forms and senses remain supplied unless the saved Unit6 contract explicitly introduces them. For example, **hūse** is not an earlier vocabulary item; the whole phrase **tō his hūse** needs local support.

The new productive source targets are **wolde** with a plain infinitive of volition, the imperative forms **Nim** and **gā**, and the morphology of **tō bēodanne / tō sęllanne**. The prefixed lexical infinitive **gecȳðan** remains supplied. The source’s “have something available to offer/give” frame remains supplied and must not be translated as an obligation merely because modern English uses “have to.” Cook §200 quotes the first of these exact source phrases.

Supply the complete opening temporal/content structure, the source prepositional phrases, both relative constructions, and the participial **cweðende**. In particular **intō ðǣre ceastre** has an actual dative; the task should not substitute an accusative or infer a general rule from motion. The definite gift clause permits the ordinary indicative reading of **hæbbe** adopted here, but its surface is syncretic. The supplied free-relative clause makes a unique mood-identification exercise unnecessary. Cook §§194–197 distinguish doubt/attraction from definite assertion; the claim is contextual, not a diagnosis from the ending alone.
