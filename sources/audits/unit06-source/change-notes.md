# Transcription and excerpt boundaries

The source is Cook's selected 1895 second edition, printed167 / PDF183. The exact continuous selection begins with Ðā, sōna swā on editorial prose line3 and ends after ceastre. on printed line10. The whole source episode extends from p166 to p167 and is **not** assigned in its entirety. The selected subepisode narrates the fisherman's care, his gift of half his cloak, and his first complete spoken instruction. The subsequent forecasts, conditions, petition and oath remain outside the extract.

`source-lines.txt` preserves the selected printed line divisions and all three attached note cues. `source-text.txt` joins the lines and removes those cues; it preserves the opening speech quotation without inventing a source closing mark. `learner-excerpt.txt` adds a closing quotation immediately after ceastre. to mark the editorial end of the quotation. That added mark is the only difference between the two running texts. The learner title/context must call this an excerpt and explicitly state that it ends after the fisherman's first spoken instruction.

The source's lexical spelling, quantities, hooked ę, punctuation and capitalization are retained. In particular **læg** and **geseah** remain unmarked, **gīet** has a printed macron, and the actual prefixed finite verb is **ūpāhōf**. No short vowel is silently lengthened. No linguistic word is replaced, and no line-end hyphen is removed within this selection. Typographic quote style is represented by Unicode curly quotes; the space before the printed colon is regularized.

The contextual lead-in may state that Apollonius survived the shipwreck in the earlier reading, encountered an old fisherman, and asked for help. That is editorial English context, not translated or omitted Old English silently spliced into this passage.
