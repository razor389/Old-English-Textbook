Ðā, sōna swā se fiscere geseah ðæt se geonga mann æt his fōtum læg, hē mid mildheortnesse hine ūpāhōf, and lǣdde hine mid him tō his hūse, and ðā ēstas him beforan lęgde ðe hē him tō bēodanne hæfde. Ðā gīet hē wolde, be his mihte, māran ārfæstnesse him gecȳðan; tōslāt ðā his wǣfels on twā, and sealde Apollonie ðone healfan dǣl, ðus cweðende: “Nim ðæt ic ðē tō sęllanne hæbbe, and gā intō ðǣre ceastre.”
