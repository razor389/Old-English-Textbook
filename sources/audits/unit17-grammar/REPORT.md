## Material linguistic decisions

- Direct-address adjectives in 37 follow Cook §55 and the actual earlier address rule: `wæstmbǣran trēowu`, `gefiðredan fuglas`, `Geongan`, and `ealdan`. The quantifier `eall` follows its separate strong pattern. `eall his mægnu` and `eall folc` are neuter plural. Strong `wæstmbǣru` and feminine plural `-e` alternatives are not globally condemned; their grammatical validity and compliance with the selected taught pattern are separate questions.
- The explicit hālga citation in 37.14 is the weak masculine noun; the supplied phrase uses `hālgena`, not adjective `hālgra`. Dense genitive and figurative relations are supplied whole.
- Cook §188 permits a simple preterite for the English past/perfect distinctions. Thus 37.6 need not acquire an untaught auxiliary-perfect frame merely because its first English clause lacks an `om. hath` direction. This general selection rule is explicitly new production, not mislabeled prior teaching.
- `onfōn` in 39.6 receives a dative reward; 40.8 accepts a dative person into care. Cook §164j and inspected BT entries support the selected frame. Other attested cases can be valid language without meeting that target. English “accepted” remains the required meaning; “helped” is not substituted as the compulsory translation.
- In 42.1 love is the nominative subject, the lover is genitive, and the girl is the accusative goal after `on`. `befēoll` retains the exercise's explicit be-prefix, although the original reading outside the assigned span has `gefēoll`.
- The whole 144,000 expression in 42.2 is `būton ðæt hundtēontig and fēowertig and fēower ðūsendo`, independently inspected in registered BT 1082 after the coordinator's discovery. Acute length signs are normalized to macrons, a line-division hyphen is joined, and `ðūsendo` is retained. No dative analysis or general būton government rule is inferred.
- The singular collective `męnigu` and subsequent plural participants in 42.3 are explained inside the whole supplied phrase. This is not a general permission to disregard agreement. The selected 42.8 knee is singular `cnēo`; the alternative stem is independently evidenced, not silently mixed with the taught paradigm.
- Negative `ne witon` in 43.8 uses the assigned finite system. The reading's contracted `nāst` is supplied. Actual prior `u15-negative-concord` is reused with a current contextual note, without rewriting its earlier description.
- `gelǣred` in reading token 121 and `gelǣrede` in 43.13 use the actual complete Unit 16 lexical adjective `gelared` and its educated/trained sense. They do not require newly invented lǣran participle cells.
- The last semantic pass selected actual prior `gefyllan-fill-source` in 41.2, `cleopian-u16-cry` in 43.3, and `slean-kill` in 43.16.
