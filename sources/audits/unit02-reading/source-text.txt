Eornostlice ðā wǣron fullfręmede heofonas and eorðe and eall hiera frætwung. And God ðā gefylde on ðone seofoðan dæg his weorc ðe hē geworhte, and hē geręste hine on ðone seofoðan dæg fram eallum ðǣm weorce ðe hē gefręmede. And God geblētsode ðone seofoðan dæg and hine gehālgode, for-ðon-ðe hē on ðone dæg geswāc his weorces ðe hē gescēop tō wyrceanne.
