[p124.4] God cwæð[8] ðā ęft,[9] “Geweorðe[10] nū fæstnes tōmiddes
[p124.5] ðǣm[3] wæterum,[11] and tōtwǣme[12] ðā[3] wæteru[11] fram ðǣm
[p124.6] wæterum.” And God geworhte ðā fæstnesse, and tōtwǣmde
[p124.7] ðā wæteru ðe[13] wǣron under ðǣre fæstnesse fram ðǣm ðe[13]
[p124.8] wǣron bufan ðǣre fæstnesse; hit wæs ðā swā gedōn.[14] And
[p124.9] God hēt ðā fæstnesse Heofonan.[15] And wæs ðā geworden
[p124.10] ǣfen and morgen ōðer[16] dæg.
