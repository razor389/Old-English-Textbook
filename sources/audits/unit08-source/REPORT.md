## Exercise fidelity and dependencies

`exercises-prompts.json` contains the exact seventeen original prompts, preserving I.8.8 `(168.1)`, I.8.10 `(themselves)`, I.8.13 `(gesęte)`, and I.8.14 `(sing.)` and `[in]`. The only prompt typography changes are removal of printed line wrapping and the line-end split of *indeed*.

`exercise-requirements.json` gives an independently prepared model for each prompt, selected form and sense IDs, exact actual prior records, contextual requirements, and explicit new teaching or supply requirements. The principal additions are:

- All four previously recognition-only plural cells of **līf**, in a complete singular/plural display, plus the cognate accusative **libban one's lives**. Item8 points to Cook §168.1, not §163.1.
- Explicit promotion of **gefyllan** in the **fill** sense; earlier productive **finish/accomplish** does not cover it.
- Intransitive weak II **ætēowian appear**. Cook124 line12 has **ætēowie drȳgnes**; Cook275 distinguishes this lemma from transitive **ætīewan reveal/display**. The required complete assigned system belongs in the contract.
- Exact **gesęte** remains supplied in item13. Cook125 line15 attests placement with **on + dative location**, supporting the bounded fish-in-the-sea adaptation without a general new goal rule.
- **habban + accusative controlled object + on possessive dative gewealde**: direct source witness at Cook127 line9. Ordinary spatial *on* or *under* did not teach this control frame.
- The actual register has **no productive scīnan subjunctive system** and **no geblētsian imperative cells**. Complete selected subjunctive systems and the imperative pair must be explicit new teaching, despite familiarity with the lemmas and other systems.
- Item17 requires second-person singular agreement in the relative after **ðū**. Third-singular substitution is an error.

The earlier productive forms for **weorc**, **gēar**, **weaxan**, **ēac**, adverbial **swilce**, and **sōðlice truly/indeed** are reused; they are not duplicate new milestones. Selected neuter plural possessives are **ūru** and **ēowru**. Selected **trēo** and **wæteru** remain the model forms.

The excerpt begins **Hwæt sægst ðū, mangere?** and ends **unēaðe cwic ætberstende.** It contains a complete what/how exchange and the merchant's explanation of foreign trade and its risks. The following commodity catalogue and later profit discussion were compared and excluded from this proposed bounded span; only the contract makes the final assignment.

The exact Old English is unchanged. Four original English speaker labels are retained as paratext and excluded from the 72-token ledger. The principal teaching targets are direct **hwæt/hū** questions and relative **ðā**: accusative neuter plural antecedent **ðing**, but nominative neuter plural relative subject inside its own clause. Twelve adjacent local helps cover the content conjunction **ðæt**, **behēfe + dative**, correlative **ge ... and**, substantival **weligum**, nautical forms, the exact plural predicate participle, collective **hit**, the bring/recipient frame, circumstance **mid**, shipwreck and loss, and **unēaðe cwic ætberstende**. Familiar morphology does not promote these new constructions.

Two source-only limits are now explicit in the local helps. **And hū?** abbreviates *And how [are you useful]?* by reference to the preceding answer; this ellipsis is supplied, not a general production outcome. In **ofer sǣ**, accusative extent is a contextual interpretation: **sǣ** is singular accusative/dative syncretic, so its surface cannot establish a unique case. The ledger retains both prior form candidates, and the source tasks assess the meaning *across the sea* rather than demand a unique case label. The exact negated passive **ne bēoð ācęnnede** is also supplied as a unit, including **ne**; familiarity with the negative particle does not independently assign that whole construction.

It was accepted without repair as “The man whose ship I saw is good.” All eight forms were verified as previously productive; the new scope is the already selected genitive-relative function. This later check is recorded separately in `composition-independent-check.json` and does not alter the chronology of the initial fifteen-example review.
