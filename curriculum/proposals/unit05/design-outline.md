## Governing scope

Teach complete present and preterite indicative paradigms for the selected strong IV–VII systems, together with their ordinary uses and exceptions. The grammar analyst's provisional list has 29 IV–VII systems: IV **beran, niman, cuman**; V **sprecan, cweðan, giefan, biddan, licgan, sittan, etan, sēon, geseon**; VI **faran, slēan, standan, hębban, scieppan, gescieppan, swęrian**; VII **feallan, healdan, weaxan, cnāwan, hēawan, flōwan, wēpan, lǣtan, hātan, fōn**. Exact forms and productive versus reference status await the reconciled grammar audit. Proposed exceptional **gefeon/hliehhan** references must stay outside independent recall if adopted.

The complete Cook I.5 set also requires four new whole systems from earlier classes: **geswīcan, scīnan, flēogan, swimman**. These are not prior productive vocabulary. The first has only a supplied preterite and genitive construction; the second only supplied subjunctives; the last two are absent. Teach all twelve finite cells of each before assigning Cook's required present or past forms. The earlier class features can be reused without falsely treating these new lexemes as old.

Four principal parts are displayed with exact source evidence. Infinitive and past-participle entries remain supplied citations. A task may identify their positions with the entries visible; it must not require unaided nonfinite recall or use infinitive, participial, perfect, passive, imperative or subjunctive syntax. Finite first/third singular and plural principal parts support productive indicative formation only.

Full morphology has a separate lexical-use scope. Independently evidenced frames are needed for carry/take/see/strike/hold plus accusative, give plus accusative thing and dative recipient, ordinary eat, intransitive sit/lie/stand/fall, grow/fly/shine/swim, and **geswīcan + genitive**. Diagnostic-only verbs can receive form practice without invented complements. Speech with **cweðan** needs an explicit extension of the earlier direct-quotation construction; content clauses remain supplied. Active **hātan** morphology does not establish anomalous **hātte**, commands with infinitives, or the first-day double-accusative naming frame.

All nouns in Cook I.5 already have complete productive paradigms and the required senses. Existing nouns also suffice for a controlled account. No new noun class is necessary merely to make a vocabulary quota. The alternative weak feminine **heofone** remains actual-form reading support if its complete paradigm is not deliberately introduced. If later practice adds a new noun, its whole relevant singular/plural paradigm, sense and ordinary use must be recorded before authorship.

## Practice structure

Use substantial new practice because the sixteen source sentences neither represent every class nor exercise all person/number and stem contrasts. The provisional sequence is:

1. Four supported identification/contrast tasks: principal-part positions; strong versus weak preterite; singular versus plural stem and the second-singular preterite; finite morphology versus a supported nonfinite/source construction.
2. About twenty compact form-production tasks, each with two to four explicit person/number/tense targets. Together they reach every productive lexical system and every class/subpattern, including contracted presents, present second/third-singular changes, V **etan**, nasal **niman/cuman**, VI **standan**, j-presents, VII long versus short past contrasts and the four bridge verbs. Every requested cell is explicit; a list of headwords is not adequate coverage.
3. Eight varied clause tasks: controlled English-to-OE, tense/person transformations, object/recipient case choice, negative statements, and direct quotation if independently evidenced. Nominative, accusative, dative, genitive, agreement, possession, adjectives and number are reused in the actual new verb frames. Include human first/second persons and dual subjects, not only third-person narratives.
4. The full sixteen Cook prompts as a coherent consolidation set, with only the declared local help in item6.
5. A connected new account of sharing food and watching birds/fish, using taught nouns, finite indicative actions, pronoun reference and coordination. Four or five tasks test comprehension, exact parsing, a bounded person/tense change and translation. Its exact OE, all-token dependencies and intended meanings are frozen only after morphology and frames are reconciled.
6. First-day revisit tasks and complete second-day source tasks: selective finite analysis, comprehension and supported translation. Every printed source token and construction gets an exact support or teaching status. No task demands unaided production of the remaining wishes, passives, clauses or resultative formulae.
7. A final short contrast/diagnosis set to expose productive versus supplied knowledge and class contrasts without making source-dependent syntax an independent production requirement.

Task counts are planning estimates. Exact prompts, IDs, target records, evidence, grading obligations and all dependency links will be recorded in `task-plan.json` before the coordinator freezes the contract. No table-only display will be misreported as assessment of every cell.
