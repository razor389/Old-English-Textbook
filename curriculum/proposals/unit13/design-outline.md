# Unit 13 design proposal

Unit 13 develops **clause order and reference** from the twelve reviewed active units. The actual contracts and cumulative registers at `e17fc5f` establish prior teaching: 647 lemmas, 8,235 forms, 778 senses and 599 construction records. This proposal is a contract input; the coordinator must reconcile it with the independent source and grammar audits and save the contract before either learner or key master is written.

Keep all **52 Cook Group VII prompts**, Exercises 22–25, in their original sets of fifteen, thirteen, twelve and twelve items. Preserve every original ID, English word, bracket, number control and section reference. Editorial directions and local helps are separate. In particular, the explicit **“verb last”** instruction in 25.7 is that task’s target; it does not turn a general ordering tendency into a universal law.

## Teaching sequence

1. First teach the small core of new vocabulary used in the examples and connected account, with its complete selected paradigms. Then begin with the finite verb, its subject, and the complete constituents of its clause. Reuse the actual case, agreement, relative, content and adverbial systems already taught. A noun phrase can contain several words; “second constituent” does not mean “second written word.” Case and agreement remain decisive when a non-subject phrase comes first.
2. Compare subject-first main clauses with a fronted object or adverbial and a following finite verb. Distinguish a coordinator from an initial constituent. Explain personal-pronoun placement and print the checked counterexample **Æfter ðon hē underðēodde** with local help and its exact source locator. The fragment is evidence for the possibility of early pronouns, not an additional unannounced reading or productive verb system.
3. Compare main and dependent clause tendencies. A late finite verb is common in relative, content and adverbial clauses, while actual prose permits other positions. Do not teach a mechanical operation that moves the English verb to the end, or imply that a grammatical alternative fails merely because it differs from a model. A stated exercise target can nevertheless require one particular arrangement.
4. Develop reference tracking beyond the single sentence. Gender, number and case constrain a pronoun’s analysis; lexical meaning, discourse and explicit nouns help identify its referent. A masculine pronoun can refer to a masculine song. More than one antecedent can remain possible; the nearest noun is not a rule. Use an explicit noun phrase when a composition task requires unambiguous reference.
5. Revisit coordination and quotation with reference in view. A repeated subject or copula may be omitted in the bounded familiar frames; recover the actual agreement if it is supplied. Quoted first and second person belong to the quotation’s speaker and addressee. Reported person and time follow their explicitly stated context.
6. Consolidate relative antecedent versus internal function, and distinguish temporal **ðā**, correlative **ðā** and article **ðā**. Nest a short relative inside a temporal clause, then return to the main clause. This provides a practical long-sentence procedure without teaching a new morphology merely from a word’s position.
7. Teach the ordinary new lexical senses and complete defined nominal, adjectival and verbal systems required by all four Cook sets. New kinship and exceptional noun patterns require literal complete tables. Repeated familiar families may use visible shared models and explicit stems, provided every derived cell has a complete reproducible route. Command, infinitive and participle systems are assigned separately from indicative morphology. Source variants and supplied frames are not promoted by appearance in a table.
8. Move from complete paradigm practice and diagnostic forms to clause analysis, controlled composition and all four Cook sets. The original tasks carry the unit’s central order and reference work; many Cook items primarily consolidate earlier syntax with new vocabulary.
9. Read the connected new account before the exact Chad passage. Use the source’s actual long sentences, speaker shift, sound reference, signal and summons. Its difficult constructions remain visibly supported. The fronted **Ðā stefne and ðone sang** is governed by the embedded **gehīerde**, as Cook’s note 8 specifies; it must not be described as an ordinary object of **cwæð**.

## Practice design

The design has **90 tasks**: 52 Cook prompts and 38 originals. There are six substantial morphology tasks, twelve analysis tasks, eight controlled compositions, four tasks on the new account and eight source-reading tasks. Each task lists its actual learner inputs and every requested output position. Public dependency sets are unordered; ordered output models and complete translation witnesses are private.

Morphology practice includes complete selected kinship tables; complete **æx** and **ēagðȳrel** tables; noun-stem diagnostics; a complete strong **swēte** table with properly bounded weak comparative and superlative diagnostics; a complete **forceorfan** indicative; complete selected command pairs and participle-base pairs. The six tasks request 101 actual form positions, together with their explanatory outputs. The forceorfan principal parts remain visible; explaining the finite stems does not test recall or use of its unassigned nonfinite forms.

The display plan covers 76 defined systems and 1,008 cells in 43 literal matrices with explicit shared-model routes: 562 cells are literal and 446 are derived. The shared verbal model is restricted to six checked weak II indicative stems, using a full fordilgian matrix and exact stem substitution. Other finite systems remain complete literal matrices. Nominal sharing is likewise restricted to the audited matching inflectional family. Sharing an inflectional model never transfers a lexical sense, comparison meaning or syntactic use.

Plan several study sessions. Establish core vocabulary and clause analysis first; work through the remaining nominal and verbal groups with morphology practice and Cook 22–23; then undertake the longer Cook 24–25 sentences and controlled compositions. The new account consolidates the reference procedure before the exact source. Learners can return to the full reference tables while practising a newly introduced system.

Analysis distinguishes case roles under fronting, three functions of **ðā**, relative embedding, continued subjects, masculine sound reference, genuine ambiguous reference, quoted person, reported person, omitted copulas and nested clauses. Composition makes the target order explicit without grading every alternative as bad language. The final synthesis asks learners to explain both the limits of general order rules and Cook’s specific target.

## Readings

The new account, **The bishop and the monk**, is currently 87 words. Its length follows its complete hearing, inquiry, summons and gathering. It is new teaching material, not a historical report or adaptation of Chad. It uses explicit participants, a song’s masculine pronoun, temporal correlation, relative embedding, a continued coordinated subject and direct-speech reference. It deliberately does not invent an answer to the monk’s question. The final model and token bindings follow independent checks of every form, ordinary sense and frame.

The exact source is **The Passing of Chad: the music and summons**, Cook *First Book*, printed 152–153, one-based PDF 168–169, editorial line 152.3 through 153.9 partial, ending **and ðū ēac swilce mid wes.** The accepted transcription has 236 words and all 24 attached original notes. The sentence begins with Owini’s outside work, contrasts the absent companions and the bishop in the oratory, follows the sound’s arrival and return, and closes with the signal and summons. This is a coherent episode; the subsequent exhortation and death remain outside the assigned span.

An English context bridge explains Owini’s manual work, his companions’ usual study and the distinction between the local oratory and communal church. It does not imply that unprinted preceding Old English has been taught. Preserve exact source **ciricean**, **ęft**, **hwæthwugu**, **nēalǣcton**, **singendra** and other variants; ordinary productive spellings have separate identities where needed. Supply the actual source forms and constructions at their points of use, including the anticipatory fronting, preposition stranding and larger participial/perception frames.

The source tasks require a complete supported translation, a clause-and-subject map of the opening, sound-reference tracking, explicit actor changes, the stranded preposition, the fronted embedded objects, quoted reference in the summons, and a learner-selected comparison of actual clause orders. No task demands recall of a supplied form or derives a universal syntactic rule from one printed example.

## Scope and review boundaries

Full paradigms establish only their recorded morphological scope. Ordinary senses and lexical frames need their own evidence and teaching. Existing **hīeran** does not teach prefixed **gehīeran**; existing **sprecan** finite forms do not teach its infinitives or a conversational prepositional frame. The previous supplied **ðǣr** and **ęft** need explicit promotion if used independently. The audits have already exposed these boundaries; final inventory and task bindings must resolve them before drafting.

Keep the indefinite **hwæthwega** expression and long biblical phrases locally supplied where the broader systems would distract from the grammatical unit. Retain all original prompts. Supply actual supported forms and meanings, not headwords alone. Ordinary useful vocabulary can be newly productive without assigning every idiom containing it. No fixed vocabulary ceiling is imposed.

The source editor owns exact transcription and nominal evidence; the grammar analyst owns clause-order, verbal and frame evidence; design owns this sequence, practice inputs, display routes, dependency reconciliation and private answer seeds. Root owns the brief, coverage contract, Beads, shared indexes, registers and final integration. The later independent reviewer is fresh and authors no graded task. During blind solving the reviewer receives only the actual learner master, accepted earlier units and registers, and necessary original page images. All current proposals, audits, contract analyses, annotated source tokens, private models and full translation witnesses are withheld until the learner solutions are frozen.

The public files are design and evidence inputs. No learner or answer-key prose is authorized before root’s reconciled contract checkpoint. Complete only the requested Unit 13.
