Status: ready for coordinator contract. The final grammar audit and complete source audit have been reconciled; `validation.json` passes. No learner/key prose has been written by the design analyst.

## Prior boundary

The actual reviewed contracts of Units 1 and 2 and the current cumulative registers have been read. They contain 102 lemmas, 770 form records, 108 senses and 81 constructions. Only those recorded recognition/production milestones count.

Adjectives, ordinals and several source constructions encountered in earlier readings remain supplied. In particular, gōd, īdel, ǣmtig, ān, eall and seofoðan are not prior productive vocabulary. The first/second-person personal genitives are recognition-only citations; their agreeing possessive adjectives require new lexical/form/construction records. The ordinary invariant possessors his/hiere/hiera, personal object cases, dual agreement, complete eom/wæs indicative and 22 affirmative weak indicative systems are prior productive knowledge. Negative nabban remains recognition-only.

The plural morphology of lēoht was recognized in Unit 1 but not independently produced. Counted lights/luminaries need an explicit sense decision and promotion of the required plural forms. Ordinary light as a mass noun does not settle that new use.

## Proposed progression

1. Complete strong and weak adjective paradigms, all genders and singular/plural cases, with distinct masculine/neuter instrumental singular cells for recognition. Teach short/long stem endings, æ/a alternation, assigned disyllabic syncope, and -e adjectives together. Contrast attributive agreement, predicate agreement and the ordinary determiner conditions. Source exceptions remain explicit.
2. Complete agreeing possessives mīn, ðīn, uncer, incer, ūre and ēower, contrasting the possessor encoded by their stem with agreement determined by the possessed noun. Keep their records separate from personal-genitive citations and from invariant his/hiere/hiera. Do not state that every adjective after a possessive must be weak.
3. Regular comparison, an audited umlaut series and the necessary irregular series, with full comparative weak and superlative strong/weak morphology. Introduce a bounded ðonne comparison of parallel nominative subjects with an understood copula. Avoid genitive standards, comparative clauses with new verbs, and advanced quantity syntax.
4. Ordinary adjective-derived adverbs and their comparison through a compact checked set. Distinguish an adverb modifying an action or degree from adjective agreement. Lexical or citation-only examples do not create unassigned syntactic knowledge.
5. Cardinals 1–12: complete ān singular, twēgen and ðrīe systems, then the usual invariant adjectival counting forms 4–12 with plural count nouns. Plural ān is recognition/reference only, without an assigned plural sense of cardinal one. Ordinals first–seventh receive their complete relevant patterns and exceptional conditions; ōðer remains strong even after the article. Add the necessary se/ðes demonstrative systems. Cardinal noun agreement is distinct from dual personal-pronoun agreement. Higher numerals and their partitive-genitive constructions remain unassigned.
6. Substantial new form, case-choice, translation, reference and connected-comprehension tasks; all exact Cook I.4.1–17 prompts; a controlled descriptive/enumerative account using learned finite verbs; and a coherent source fourth-day Creation reading with precise local support.

## Deliverables and integration

The final proposal uses the Unit 2 integration structure:

- `inventory.json`: 80 lemma records, 1,567 explicit form records, 84 senses, 46 features, 78 paradigm records, 205 teaching/support IDs, all 109 source tokens, evidence and actual prior requirements.
- `task-plan.json`: 70 exact tasks (53 new plus 17 Cook), each with separate required/tested form, sense and feature IDs and a key counterpart. Its target sequences witness closed solutions; they are analysis specifications, not a substitute for an independently prepared key.
- `compositions.json`: the exact connected account (61 words) and ten ungraded teaching examples (53 words), exhaustively linked to productive form/sense records. Neither controlled material nor its examples require supplied lexical forms or senses.
- `prior-audit.json`: the actual reviewed Unit 1–2 boundary and file identities.
- `validation.json`: passing reference, exact-source, token, status, evidence-image, uniqueness, paradigm and NFC checks.

The `display_plan` makes the full morphology teachable without requiring 78 repeated tables. Full representative declensions, explicit stem/ending mappings and diagnostic cells must uniquely recover every selected form. Six comparative series include their exceptional genitive plurals; all six possessives need complete recoverable patterns. Eall is strong-only recognition/reference, without independent production. Instrumental forms and plural ān remain nonproductive. The four lēoht plural cells are promoted through the complete checked heavy-neuter pattern for the new counted-luminary sense; N/A plural are directly assessed.

New source evidence is namespaced `U03-S-*`; existing article/form IDs and earlier supplied exposure histories are retained. Source-category and accepted-variant fields survive the grammar conversion. Source spellings and alternatives are not silently regularized. The task plan distinguishes valid alternative language from the requested selected target.

The production gate caught a proposed contrast with recognition-only eorðe genitive plural; the final comparison uses previously productive **steorrena**. Task contexts also resolve nominative/accusative ambiguity, distinguish identical final -e in Mīne/Ūre, avoid an unsupported weak-after-genitive rule, and keep adverb practice free of unintroduced comparative-clause ellipsis.
