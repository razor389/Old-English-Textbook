## Prior teaching boundaries

The fifteen actual active contracts and the actual cumulative registers at baseline `e1d94be` govern. Prior coverage is granular: an earlier lemma, table, quotation or source gloss does not establish a new sense, mood, government frame or construction.

- Unit 2 teaches complete selected personal forms including duals, plural finite agreement with dual subjects, contextual reference, and direct quotation after *sęcgan*.
- Unit 5 extends direct quotation to *cweðan*. Unit 6 supplies the selected irregular indicative, imperative and infinitive systems; it distinguishes *cunnan* learned ability from *witan* knowledge. Its fisherman availability/complement constructions remain local support unless later independently promoted.
- Units 7–8 teach the assigned subjunctives, elementary passive, contextual mood choices, questions, relatives and their internal case. Their complete tables retain lexical and syntactic limits.
- Unit 9 teaches bounded knowing/saying content clauses, dependent questions, *hātan* command and *gesēon* perception infinitives, with an accusative actor distinct from the subject of a finite content clause.
- Unit 10 teaches selected case government and experiencers. Unit 11 teaches the selected temporal, causal, purpose, result, concessive and conditional frames. Neither licenses unrestricted lexical government.
- Unit 12 teaches temporal relations in narrative, selected transitive *habban* compounds and simultaneous/anterior/intended-later reports without mechanical English backshift. Its intransitive *wesan* compounds and present-participle periphrases retain their specified recognition limits.
- Unit 13 teaches clause-order tendencies, constituent roles, participant reference, ambiguity and recovery of coordinated predicates. A nearest-noun pronoun rule and mandatory verb-final transformation would both contradict that teaching.
- Unit 15 teaches negative concord, selected negative indicative systems, indefinite/distributive/free-choice reference and comparison. Supplied attracted relative case and unassigned negative subjunctives remain separate.

Exact register IDs, prior milestones and required contextual refinements belong in the binding packet, not in broad claims of cumulative mastery.

## Proposed sequence

1. Re-establish the actor, addressee, clause boundaries, finite verbs and temporal viewpoint of an extended passage. Use short translated examples after their actual prerequisites.
2. Present each genuinely new lexical sense and government frame with its full assigned nominal or verbal system. Familiar nominal classes may use an explicit prior complete model and diagnostic stem; new classes and newly introduced verbal systems receive complete defined tables. Distinguish new production, new recognition, supplied and prior records.
3. Practise distinctive new forms together with ordinary use. Isolated form outputs should serve actual contrasts and missing Cook coverage, rather than mechanically repeat every table cell. Reference-only positions are identification tasks with their table visible.
4. Practise direct quotation, reported content, commands and their execution, perception versus finite complements, reference ambiguity, and contextually simultaneous/anterior/later reports. Require an explicit output for each requested decision and keep solution wording out of response labels.
5. Retain the five complete Cook sets. Their internal connected clusters are useful: 32.6–7 dual fishers; 33.1–3 command, fulfillment and quoted prophecy; 34.11–13 speaker/addressee/Father reference and *cunnan/witan*; 35.7 layered relative/passive/reporting; 36.2–6 contrasting actions and grounds for forgiveness. The full grammar and vocabulary precede independent composition; local helps remain visible.
6. Read the gymnasium scene with exact transcription, separate original apparatus and modern supports. Translate the whole selection, then track participants, connect clauses, analyse selected moods and temporal relations, and explain the essential narrative progression.

## Source-control and assessment traps

Printed 169 note 6 calls the king's knowledge/equal clause unclear. Do not grade a single antecedent as morphologically certain. Preserve the actual Old English; distinguish a defensible interpretation from the Latin offered in the note.

Printed 169 note 10 observes that the Old English *top* passage departs from the Latin account of rubbing him with ointment. Do not silently replace the Old English action with the Latin. Supply the relevant Latin meaning in English, the lexical/contextual difficulty, and a translation that preserves the actual Old English wording and records the uncertainty. No Latin knowledge is assumed.

Printed 170 note 15 identifies mixed direct and indirect constructions in the invitation. Preserve the shifts of *him*, *ðē* and *ðū*, identify who is speaking to whom at each level, and keep this as a supported source-local construction. A separately composed regular report may be used for comparison, clearly labelled new teaching material.

Cook 32.7 omits English *will* and expressly requests dual *ye*. Cook 33.3 requires the complete movement, purpose/fulfillment and prophecy sequence. Cook 34.7 and 34.9 require contextual future reference without automatic auxiliary insertion. Cook 35.4–5 need substitute *dōn* rather than an unexamined gloss *do*. Cook 36.2–5 contrast agents, omitted auxiliaries and negative/positive actions; a fluent abbreviated model would fail the prompt.

No final task roster or dependency closure is asserted until both independent evidence packets stabilize. The final proposal must bind ordered inputs, every output position, form/sense/feature IDs, paradigm coordinates or derivation routes, model targets, essential meaning, valid alternatives and relevant errors.
