## Grammatical progression

1. Contrast the strong stem series with the already taught weak dental preterite. Explain the four principal-part positions and distinguish the displayed infinitive and participle citations from independent nonfinite syntax.
2. Give all present and preterite indicative person/number cells. Compact display may combine the three identical plural persons and first/third preterite singular where they actually coincide; each cell remains separately recorded. The strong preterite second singular uses its own audited stem and ending. Prior dual subjects use the appropriate plural verb.
3. Teach Classes I, II and III with their necessary present second/third-singular changes, ablaut series, consonant alternations, contracted patterns and important lexical exceptions together. Give complete representative models and explicit lexical substitutions from which every selected cell can be recovered. Preserve checked alternatives without creating recall milestones for every spelling.
4. Apply the forms to ordinary affirmative clauses and present/past narration. Extend simple preverbal negation only where the source audit supports noncontracting strong finite verbs. Keep lexical government separate from morphological class.
5. Reuse actual prior noun, article, pronoun, possessive, adjective and simple coordination systems in meaningful case/person/number choices, comprehension and bounded composition.
