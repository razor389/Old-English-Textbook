# Unit 6 design proposal

This is a pre-contract curriculum proposal, not learner prose or a teaching milestone. The coordinator owns the active contract and must reconcile this design with the independent grammar and source audits before authorship.

The five actual active contracts and cumulative registers support 80 productive indicative verb lexemes, but no productive or recognition infinitive milestones and no imperative forms. All 170 earlier records identified as infinitive/participle positions are supplied citations or local reading help. The detailed comparison in `prior-audit.json` finds no contracted form, sense or construction missing from the actual registers. The Cook crosswalk remains 79 assigned and 565 deferred items.

## Systems to teach together

1. Complete present/preterite indicative systems of witan, cunnan, ðurfan, durran, sculan, mugan and mōtan, with practical senses and their elementary permitted argument/complement frames. Cook's mugan label and the common label magan must be distinguished explicitly. Unattested or uncertain infinitive citations are labelled rather than made recall targets.
2. Complete dōn, gān and willan indicative systems, plus the six alternate bēon present-indicative cells under the existing wesan lemma. Teach suppletive gān/ēode and irregular dōn/dyde with the cells. The older eom/wæs system remains available without rewritten milestones.
3. Complete second-singular/second-plural imperative pattern pairs for learned weak I–III and strong I–VII systems, including weak geminate/short/-ian types, weak III exceptions, strong contracted stems and j-presents. Full pattern coverage and selected lexical recall are distinct. Every displayed model and every exact lexical imperative demanded by a task needs an explicit new form record. There is no obligation to assign all 80 earlier lexemes merely because their indicatives were learned.
4. Plain and inflected infinitive forms for the selected useful lexical verbs, with all necessary contractions and stem changes. Teach plain infinitives after the newly assigned modal/volitional verbs and elementary purposive tō+inflected infinitive. Preserve participle citation limits. Do not construct modal infinitives or modal commands just to fill a regular grid.
5. Elementary commands addressed to one or several people, including bounded negative commands only if the audit supports them; literal cweðan quotation can now contain taught commands. The source's wishes/optatives are not relabelled as imperative morphology.

The coordinator adopts the seven preterite-present systems above. Āgan, unnan, dugan and gemunan remain outside this unit's assignment unless the actual source requires them. Negative nillan/nytan are possible recognition companions only after complete source evidence; no earlier nabban promotion is implied.

## Practice and connected material

The target is approximately 70 substantial tasks, with stable NEW-U06 identifiers: complete irregular paradigms; inflection contrasts and short translations; imperative singular/plural pairs across all required patterns; direct instructions and negative commands; plain versus inflected infinitives; modal/volitional clauses; purpose phrases; controlled-dialogue comprehension and adaptation; source comprehension, exact-form analysis and translation; cumulative repair/composition. No numbered Cook set belongs here.

The controlled account uses requests made as direct commands and declarative replies. It needs no interrogative system, hypothetical politeness, indirect command, relative clause or untaught conjunction. The initial candidate is a man preparing a journey and two companions bringing food and water; commands, willingness, ability, obligation, purpose and reference can be practised in one connected situation. Exact text is kept in `composition-seed.json` while the source editor checks it; the eventual reconciled `compositions.json` will supersede the seed.

The original source is the fisherman's kindness, gift and first complete direction in Cook's Reader XII, printed 167 / PDF183, editorial lines3–10. Its 76 words follow the previously read shipwreck. The title must acknowledge the excerpt boundary. The independent source editor owns exact text, image evidence and local support. Its last quote is editorially closed with the change declared. The unit will analyse wolde + supplied gecȳðan, taught/supplied inflected infinitives bēodanne and sęllanne, and actual second-singular Nim and gā. Relative, content, temporal and participial dependencies remain explicitly supplied.

## Preserved limits

The productive list cannot silently absorb first/second personal genitives, instrumental reference forms, eall, plural ān, *sǣwa, nabban, bregdan, fēolan or diagnostic verbs' untaught government. Modal ability, permission, necessity and volition are separate assigned senses; neither tense morphology nor English translations imply a general future or counterfactual system. No automatic ge- rule, hātte, passive, resultative or subjunctive system is introduced. I.4.18 and the birds candidate remain deferred to Unit7.
