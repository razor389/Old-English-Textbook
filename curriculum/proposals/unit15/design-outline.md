# Unit 15 design proposal

Unit 15 treats negation, indefinite and distributive reference, quantities, and comparison in argument. The actual fourteen active contracts and the cumulative registers at `9129cda` establish the prior boundary: 830 lemmas, 9,710 forms, 1,017 senses and 820 constructions. This proposal is analysis for the learning contract. The coordinator must reconcile and checkpoint that contract before learner prose or the key is drafted.

Retain all fifty original Cook prompts, in the four complete sets IX.28 (12), IX.29 (11), IX.30 (13), and joint X, XI.31 (14). Preserve every original wording, parenthetic hint, reference and order control. The audit distinguishes an English auxiliary marked for omission from an Old English finite verb or mood choice; omission instructions do not authorize deleting the event, negation, participant or temporal relation from the meaning.

## Prior teaching and actual extensions

Simple preverbal **ne** is productive in the selected older weak, strong and modal systems. The **nǣfre … ne** construction is productive within the Unit 12 never/never-again scope. Unit 15 must teach other accumulated negatives explicitly. The complete **nabban** indicative is recognition-only; the earlier exact **nis**, **nāh** and **nolde** helps do not constitute negative paradigms. A newly selected full negative system needs its own complete display, evidence, ordinary use and practice.

The ordinary strong **eall**, **ǣlc**, **sum**, **ǣnig**, **gehwilc**, **swilc**, **hwilc** and interrogative **hwæðer** forms are already present with their stated uses. **Gehwā/gehwæt** has only recognition NGDA scope. The familiar **hwā** question forms do not teach indefinite anyone. **Nān**, indefinite **hwā**, and the **-hwega** compounds have only bounded supplied exposure. Cardinal **ān**, the personal genitive **uncer**, and predicative comparative forms also require new senses or constructions where this unit uses them differently.

Existing **mā** distinguishes an adverbial amount of doing and a greater number. Cook's **māre** received amount, degree **eall**, preferential **swīðor**, and distributive **ǣgðer ge … ge** must receive their own sense and construction records. Earlier **swā … swā** equal-degree comparison is not the proportional **swā … swā** encountered with support in Unit 14. The prior registers, rather than a headword match or a displayed paradigm, determine the status of every actual requirement.

## Intended lesson sequence

1. Revisit a single negative clause and distinguish sentence negation from denial of one constituent. Teach selected common contractions in complete, clearly defined systems. Place stem changes, uncontracted alternatives and lexical/mood limits beside each table.
2. Extend negative concord to a negative person or thing together with the finite negative. Contrast a positive indefinite in a question, an indefinite in a condition, and a negative indefinite in an assertion. Interpret one negative proposition without mechanically counting English negatives.
3. Give the practical negative and indefinite families, with complete applicable inflection. Distinguish adjective agreement from a substantival pronoun; distinguish singular-only pronouns, invariant words, and the defective impersonal **man** use. The explicit Unit 8 deferred families require a disposition, including reference-only variants. No paradigm is invented merely to make every family look alike.
4. Develop each/every, inclusive both…and, one/another and partitive or distributive expressions. Give unused either/neither variants an explicit reference disposition. Keep the case of a selected person separate from the genitive group. Promote personal **uncer** only where its exact partitive use is taught. Establish the assigned free-choice **swā hwā/hwæt/hwæðer swā** constructions with internal clause case and contextual mood. Any additional family member retains the exact status recorded in the inventory.
5. Contrast equal degree, greater quantity, preference and proportion. Use actual preceding lessons as prerequisites, then explicitly teach the new comparisons. Explain how an omitted repeated predicate is recovered, how pronouns obtain their cases, and why comparison does not license a new adjective sense automatically.
6. Teach every additional noun class and separately defined verbal/adjectival system required by Cook in full. Put ordinary government and basic uses beside the forms. Preserve lexical and number limits in reference cells; source-only vocabulary or rare constructions receive exact visible help instead of an unbounded new system.
7. Use purposeful form tasks, sentence analysis and controlled composition to make the new choices explicit before the complete Cook sets. Original tasks must test meaningful distinctions absent or sparse in Cook: negative concord, contracted and uncontracted negatives, each versus all, inclusive both…and, free choice between two, indefinite versus interrogative reference, and equal versus proportional comparison. Unused either/neither variants remain bounded reference rather than independent composition targets.
8. Read the three connected source selections, with comprehension and selective structural work before full translation. Track who speaks, who receives or gives, whose understanding is compared, and how a petition differs from an assertion or a prohibition. Preserve all original apparatus separately from modern help.

## Task, display and evidence rules

Every task receives a stable ID, separate key ID, exact prompt, explicit target and requested output positions. Every Old English input and selected model token binds an actual form and sense. English explanations and comprehension answers also receive output positions; they must not disappear merely because they produce no Old English form. Form tasks identify each requested paradigm cell separately. Grammar targets distinguish valid alternatives from compliance with an explicit source hint.

Complete newly introduced systems should appear as literal tables, with a complete route for every displayed cell. Previously productive tables need not be needlessly reprinted unless a comparison makes the display useful. A new sense does not require duplicate forms under invented identities. A reference-only plural or a supplied source form retains its visible limit and is excluded from independent recall.

The nominal plan distinguishes productive root-noun systems from abstract-noun reference. **Bōc**, **tōð** and **sweostor** receive complete case/number practice; **hǣtu/hǣlu** receive their evidenced singular practice with plural positions explicitly unassigned. A complete reference **strengu** system supplies the abstract-class comparison without inventing plural meanings for heat or salvation. **Engle/Dęne** have complete plural displays and explicitly unassigned singular positions. Ordinary new nouns receive full literal tables and contextual use while transferring previously taught class patterns. The productive prior spellings **wītga** and **bisceop** are reused in the Cook models.

The additional verb and adjective tables are lexical applications of established patterns, each with exact stem diagnostics and separately bounded syntax. Direct assessment of a new negative or pronominal system differs from review of an earlier conjugational or adjectival pattern applied to a new word. The practice matrix records both the exact forms actually used and the wider table positions transferred from prior patterns; a displayed cell is never counted as an extra learner answer. In F06/F07, case syncretism is an English analysis target at its prior recognition scope, while each requested Old English case form is productive.

Eight short translated teaching examples were added at the coordinator's request during reconciliation. Their 50 tokens are separately bound in `compositions.json`; each has an ungraded teaching record. They cover concord, separate and contracted negative possession, interrogative and conditional indefinite **hwā**, distribution, a genitive partitive, proportional comparison, and a negative command. Different lexical and participant choices preserve the later compositions as transfer tasks. The initial no-extra-example proposal is recorded as an earlier design stage in the reconciliation history.

Present the practice in compact blocks, with complete morphology grids, grouped analysis items, and a continuous numbered composition sequence. Named output positions are machine-readable accountability records, not an instruction to print one miniature section per response. A12 uses the complete **hēafod/fōtsceamul** tables to test lexical syncope diagnostics and vowel retention. Keep the original Cook group/exercise/item identifiers visible when arranging the complete sets.

Present the complete grammar, necessary lexical systems and vocabulary before these practice blocks. In particular, **bōc** occurs in early examples and compositions: its full root-noun table and ordinary sense must precede its first example. Give the compact vocabulary/table preparation before the examples that need it; place each example only after the construction it illustrates has been explained. The thematic practice stages in `outcomes.json` are a study guide within the fully prepared unit, not permission to postpone prerequisites until a later practice stage. Local reading and Cook helps must be visible from the start of every affected task.

Each required form, sense and feature is classified as previously taught, new production, new recognition, supplied or unresolved. A task is not closed until every unresolved requirement has been taught, supplied, revised or explicitly deferred. Local help remains visible during every affected task. Practice coverage is deliberate: it tests complete coherent patterns and contextual choices; adding isolated cells without a pedagogical reason is not a substitute for meaningful use.

Initial independent reading translations are preserved in `author-only-source-translation-witness.json`. Later source or lexical corrections belong in a separate reconciliation record. Original proposals and witnesses remain intact after the final manifest.
