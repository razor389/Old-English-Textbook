# Unit 11 design proposal

This is the curriculum analysis for **Adverbial clauses**, not the learning contract or a learner chapter. The coordinator reconciles the independently inspected sources and complete grammatical systems, then checkpoints the contract before either master is drafted. Only Unit 11 is in scope.

Retain all **46** original Cook prompts: Group IV, Exercise 16 (16 items), Exercise 17 (17 items), and Group V, Exercise 18 (13 items). Each complete set remains together, with its original group/item identifier, English wording, controls and section references. The fresh source audit corrects small-image misreadings: IV.17.13 includes *on (ofer)*, IV.17.14 begins *Ye are*, and IV.17.17 refers to *154. c*; V.18.8 includes *rose up*, V.18.10 prints *bringst*, V.18.12 says *life*, and V.18.7 supplies the exact spelling *ācenned*. Editorial directions and local help remain separate from these prompts.

## Sequence

1. Start with a clause's function, its subject and its finite verb. Distinguish a temporal or causal adjunct from the already taught content clause and relative clause. The same spelling, particularly *ðæt*, *ðā* or *ðonne*, does not establish the same function. Reuse the ordinary clause-order tendencies without teaching an obligatory transformation.
2. Teach factual past *ðā … ðā* and the selected recurring/future *ðonne … ðonne* patterns, then simultaneous *ðā-hwīle-ðe* and *mid-ðȳ*. Contrast conjunction, correlating adverb and homographic determiner/comparative uses explicitly. Teach *ǣr* and *oð-ðæt* with a contextually anticipated event; any past-viewpoint extension must stay within the analyst's explicitly evidenced boundary. Do not identify every temporal clause with one mood.
3. Extend factual cause from the actual earlier *for-ðām* scope to the separately audited selected forms. Give the reason and its consequence distinct clauses; do not call temporal succession causation.
4. Contrast intended purpose with an asserted result. Purpose *ðæt* uses the selected subjunctive; negative purpose receives its own *ðȳ-lǣs-ðe* frame. Factual *swā … ðæt* results ordinarily use the indicative. Cook §196.g's wished curse is not a rule requiring subjunctive in all result clauses. Distinguish both from content *ðæt* after the selected knowing/saying verbs.
5. Add the selected concessive pattern and the bounded contrary-present conditional. The earlier open/tentative *gif* patterns remain valid; *gif* does not impose subjunctive by itself. Make the contrary-present condition's nonpast setting explicit and contrast it with an open condition. Perfect and past-counterfactual systems remain Unit 12 or later work.
6. Introduce every necessary new lexical system in a complete defined paradigm, placing characteristic endings, stem changes and exceptions beside it. Give complete new nominal/adjectival systems or explicit full model/diagnostic routes for familiar classes. Exercise full systems and diagnostically difficult cells as well as the forms Cook happens to request. Verbal principal-part display does not assign a missing mood, nonfinite form or argument frame.
7. Teach the additional lexical frames required by the complete Cook sets. Supply narrow higher-numeral, formulaic and source-only expressions where their full system belongs later. Keep such help available at the task rather than assessing unannounced recall. Follow targeted form, contrast and controlled-composition work with all three Cook sets.
8. Finish with a connected original account using only productive material, then two exact source spans approved by the coordinator: the complete Bede metals/jet observation (Cook p.139, first four prose lines, through *onwęg*) and the complete rescue-resolution in *Æthelwald Calms the Sea* (p.142, editorial line 8, *Ðā wæs æfter langum fæce …*, through the end of p.143). A separate short English introduction supplies the sailors' preceding danger. Retain all original notes and actual local help; exact boundaries and counts belong to the source audit.

## Practice and reading design

Original tasks must cover all six adverbial functions, clause boundaries, contrasting moods in a stated context and the different functions of familiar conjunction spellings. Production prompts name the relevant meaning or communicative stance rather than demanding arbitrary transformations. Include a meaningful distinction between an intended outcome and an outcome asserted to have occurred. Include complete new paradigm work and contextual checks on the main stem alternations, with supported reference cells kept out of unaided production.

The original account will have an event, motive, action and resolution. Its first translation is independent of local source helps. Reading tasks for Bede identify the conditional relation and antecedents while keeping generic *man* and the unfamiliar jet expression supported. Sea tasks reconstruct the rescue sequence, distinguish purpose/cause/result from reported content, identify relevant finite morphology, and explain the narrative's final inference. The source participial periphrases remain supported; they do not introduce a mechanical English progressive rule. A full translation of each selected span has a separate key entry.

The fixed public practice design has **92 tasks**: eight morphology tasks, twelve clause-analysis tasks, eleven new production tasks, four controlled-reading tasks, three Bede tasks, eight sea-reading tasks and all forty-six Cook items. The fourteen translated teaching examples are ungraded. The new account, *The fisherman and his son*, has 59 words and a complete motive/action/response sequence; its vocabulary and ordinary argument frames come from actual productive records plus the new clause connections. The public examples and tasks use the established objectless *sing* and posture frames; the account uses the established objectless *eat* and transitive *carry* frames. The account explicitly names the fisherman as the first food-giver; contextual pronoun interpretation is never represented as uniquely determined by gender alone.

The exact source spans contain 35 and 195 lexical words. Bede's separate em dash is retained as punctuation, yielding 36 whitespace items for that span. Retain all 32 original notes across the spans and the earlier cross-referenced periphrasis note. Three independent full translations have been compared privately; they preserve the source's complete rescue and storm-return resolution. The later reserved reviewer will independently solve the actual tasks before seeing any of those private witnesses.

Nominal design prints the complete new *bæð*, *geat*, *seolh*, *sumer* and *winter* subpatterns, with explicit lexical diagnostics for vowel alternation, h-loss/lengthening and syncope. Familiar classes use complete visible models and a checked route for every logical cell. Both *mōna* and *sunne* have complete paradigms while their ordinary astronomical use is singular; plural reference is not an implied count-noun assignment. The selected adjective instrumental cells remain reference. Supporting verbal displays have seven complete indicative systems, five complete present/past base-participle pairs, both *gewītan* commands and the two *scēawian* infinitives. They introduce no new full subjunctive system and no unlisted participial adjective cells.

## Actual cumulative boundary

The starting revision is `fdddbe6`. The ten active contracts and full actual registers contain 447 lemmas, 6,564 forms, 519 senses and 428 constructions. Their identities, assignments, outcomes and scope limits are retained in the design's prior-state audit.

Earlier *ðā* then, *ðonne* then, *for-ðon-ðe*, purpose *ðæt*, temporal correlations and many maritime source forms have only supplied histories. Unit 7 taught open/tentative conditions and bounded subjunctive uses; Unit 9 taught factual *for-ðām* and contrary-present wishes, not all counterfactual conditions. Unit 10's *swā lange swā* remains a supplied connection with the *inasmuch as* interpretation; it is not prior temporal-clause production. Explicit new assignments must name these prior identities and preserve their histories.

Reference-only first/second personal genitives, *gehwā*, negative/indefinite families, higher numerals, the starred *sǣwa*, unassigned instrumental cells and the *unnan* present second-singular gap retain their boundaries. Productive lexical verbs do not license every preposition, infinitive, passive or complement frame. Source notes, exact text, declared normalizations and new teaching material remain distinct.

## Deliverables and independence

The source editor owns exact prompts/readings/apparatus and nominal/lexical evidence; the linguistic analyst owns verbal systems and clause/frame evidence. Design files will reconcile their public dependencies with the actual cumulative records, enumerate each task and key ID, map every full paradigm cell to a printed matrix or explicit diagnostic route, and keep private solutions and ordered expected outputs in `author-only-` files. The reserved reviewer may inspect public task text and evidence but must not open those private files before freezing independent solutions. Design readiness is evidence for the coordinator's contract, never authorization for prose by itself.
