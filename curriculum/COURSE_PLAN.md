# Course plan

Outline settled 22 October 2026; all eighteen grammar-led units completed 28 October 2026. This outline records the teaching progression and allocation of every Cook exercise; the active unit contracts establish the detailed assignments and teaching scope.

## Audience and method

Assume no Old English and familiarity with standard grammar terminology. Use concise original exposition, complete relevant declensions, and explicitly bounded complete verbal paradigms with their ordinary uses. Units may span several study sessions. Vocabulary size follows the grammatical and textual demands. Historical explanations remain short notes or later supplements.

Lambdin supplies the practical organizational model, not Old English linguistic authority; only his contents and publisher description have been inspected. Cook's selected 1895 books supply the core source material. Keep original groups, exercise and item IDs, prompts, and reversible editorial changes. Meaningful new tasks fill gaps in Cook's coverage.

The [pronunciation guide](../typesetting/pronunciation/pronunciation.pdf) precedes Unit 1 and remains available for reference. Its [separate contract](../guides/pronunciation/coverage.json) selects a practical Cook/Fulk reading convention with American English cues, plain articulation instructions, and only occasional introductory phonetic symbols. All example meanings and sentence grammar are supplied. Its 25 sound questions and separate key establish no additional vocabulary or grammatical production milestones and assign no new numbered Cook exercise items.

The [two reference glossaries](../glossaries/README.md) accompany the completed course. Old English–English covers all 38 learner/key masters, including paradigms and source notes; English–Old English covers all 1,392 chapter tasks and 25 pronunciation responses. Dictionary headwords, principal parts and selected irregular-form pointers support lookup. Reference inclusion adds no assignment or independent-recall milestone; each unit's contract still defines what is taught or supplied.

The optional [sound-history appendix](../appendices/sound-history/README.md) explains historical patterns behind the forms, including breaking, umlaut and ablaut. Its examples have supplied meanings and existing glossary headwords; source spellings have explicit lookup pointers. It introduces no assessed tasks, complete paradigms or production milestones and assigns no additional Cook material. The separate contract was saved before prose.

## How the sequence is used

Units 2, 4, and 6 provide substantial newly composed practice while the learner builds the systems needed for Cook's mixed sets. The source is not a sequence of isolated conjugation drills. The sole planned item-level split is I.4.18, “Increase (sing.) and be multiplied.”: it follows passive commands in Unit 7. All other numbered exercise sets stay together. The [complete map](COOK_MAP.md) accounts for all 48 exercises and 644 numbered items, including the joint source headings X, XI and XII, XIII.

Unit 8's [family dispositions](../sources/audits/unit08-grammar/pronoun-dispositions.json) account for every Cook §89 family. Complete morphology is given for its selected practical systems; gehwā is a singular recognition reference. Indefinite ān, negative/either expressions, indeclinable quantity words, -hwega compounds, impersonal man and free-choice clauses remain explicit Unit 15 work. Familiar interrogative or cardinal forms do not teach these additional senses and constructions. The merchant's following goods catalogue is outside Unit 8's bounded excerpt.

The later reading column identifies a progression, not an audited passage list. Exact boundaries, senses, forms, and constructions must be verified before each later contract. A needed exception receives its whole relevant pattern when introduced. Supplementary poetry requires a separate contract; this course's established spine is practical prose.

## Opening unit fixed for analysis

Unit 1 retains all 46 prompts of I.1–3. Teach the complete relevant strong and weak noun patterns, individually documented exceptions, the full article, and the present and preterite indicative **eom/wæs** series. Teach nominative subjects and noun predicates, genitive relationships, direct-object accusatives within supplied verb frames, and selected prepositional government. Provide actual accusative/dative choices and independent copular mini-passages, not tables alone.

Retain the contiguous first-day Creation narrative, FB pp.123–124 / PDF139–140, ending **ān dæg**: 69 whitespace-delimited words. Exact linguistic text is preserved; only note cues and line/page breaks are removed. Supply the actual unfamiliar forms and whole constructions locally. The guided reading does not confer productive mastery of its strong verbs, passives, wishes, adjectives or pronouns. Later units deliberately replace that support with teaching.

The unit analysis must preserve evidence limits. **God** in its proper-name sense needs no invented plural sense; **ðīestru** has no singular assigned. Wright's explicitly reconstructed genitive plural **\*sǣwa** belongs in a labelled reference cell, with no independent test or teaching milestone. Cook's **wǣstm** is corrected to **wæstm** under C01, with the original preserved. See [the settled brief](UNIT_01_BRIEF.md).
