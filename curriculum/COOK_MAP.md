Units 2, 4, and 6 contain new graded practice while their grammar prepares later mixed Cook sets. They omit no Cook set. Only Exercise 4 is divided: items 1–17 accompany adjectives in U3; **EX-I-04-18** goes to U7 because “Increase (sing.) and be multiplied.” requires both command and passive work. Its eventual contract must preserve this exact prompt. The source note for Exercise 4 refers to FB **§55**, not OCR §65.

Preserve the joint printed headings **X, XI** for Exercise 31 and **XII, XIII** for Exercises 32–48. Cook supplies no later boundary authorizing an invented division of the latter block. The headings correlate with reader selections, but Cook's preface explains that later material is freer and draws on other texts. Exact reading derivations require their own audits.

## Opening-unit evidence and source dispositions

The [Unit 1 contract](../chapters/ch01/coverage.json) assigns I.1.1–16, I.2.1–12 and I.3.1–18 without omission or altered English prompts. [Exercises 1](../reference/previous-course/sources/transcriptions/ch01/exercise-1.md) and [2](../reference/previous-course/sources/transcriptions/ch02/exercise-2.md) reuse archived transcriptions; [Exercise 3](../sources/audits/unit01-source/exercise-3.md) has a fresh complete audit. Old teaching fields do not carry over.

The selected guided reading is FB Reader I, p.123 editorial prose lines 1–5 and p.124 lines 1–3 (counted against the printed marginal 5), PDF139–140, through **ān dæg**. It comprises the entire first-day narrative. [Exact text, note cues, and normalization](../sources/audits/unit01-source/creation-first-day.md) remain separate. The old opening sentence, earth clause, and darkness clause all fall inside this new span; their historical deferments are not inherited. The previously audited lights clause (p.125 lines 12–13) and birds clause (p.126 lines 12–13) have explicit new-course deferrals in the opening contract, to Units 3 and 7 respectively, because their number/adjective or wish/passive dependencies belong to those later units.

## Audit limits

All exercise boundaries and terminal item numbers have been checked against images. Exercises 4–48 have structural, not full item-level linguistic audits. Their planned allocations may require justified item-level adjustment after actual analysis, always preserving IDs and recording every affected item. No item is silently excluded. The [course outline](COURSE_PLAN.md) sets grammatical prerequisites and intended reading progression; the [source registry](../sources/registry.json) controls editions and locators.
