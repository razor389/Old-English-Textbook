## Complete forms and bounded uses

Present all forty nominative, genitive, dative and accusative personal-pronoun cells, including first- and second-person duals. Thirty-four cells are productive. The six first-/second-person genitives are recognition only; their related agreeing possessive adjectives belong to Unit 3. Teach invariant **his, hiere, hiera**, selected by the possessor, and ordinary personal pronouns used reflexively, including the verified **geręste hine** frame. Do not imply that a pronoun's spelling determines its antecedent or that English “it” always requires neuter **hit**.

Present complete present and preterite indicative paradigms for **22 affirmative weak verbs** and the complete recognition-only negative companion **nabban**: 23 × 12 finite cells. Seven distinct surface columns may represent each paradigm if the mapping to every person/number cell is explicit. Dual subjects take the appropriate plural verb form. All four Class III verbs must appear in full. Principal-part infinitives and participles are supplied citations, with no independent nonfinite recall or syntactic milestone.

Keep the rules and exceptions beside the paradigms: long-stem Class I; its ten diagnostic dental/stem domains; irregular **sęllan/gewyrcean**; short geminate and consonant-plus-sonorant patterns; short Class I **-ian** versus Class II **-ian**; and the four distinct Class III systems. Use **ęfnan**, not impersonal **hyngran**, for the sonorant diagnostic. Preserve **geręstest** as the selected second singular but accept checked contracted **geręst**. Retain only evidenced optional variants. Do not derive full-ending alternatives mechanically from unrelated doubled consonants.

Teach independently inflected active clauses, accusative objects with checked senses, dative recipient plus accusative thing with **sęllan**, third-person possession, reflexive resting, and **ne** immediately before noncontracting finite weak verbs. The complete **nabban** system is recognition only; valid contracted/uncontracted forms are distinguished from a requested target. Negative copulas and negative concord remain outside this unit. Direct quotation with **sęcgan** is bounded to a separately complete utterance, not indirect speech.

Vocabulary is lexical, not a universal transitivity rule. **īecan, gierwan, ęfnan, hycgan** support morphological practice without an unevidenced sentence frame. **gefyllan** means “finish/accomplish a work” here; a substance-filling construction is not taught. Prefixed reading verbs are separately checked entries, not a productive rule for **ge-**.
