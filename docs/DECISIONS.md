# Decisions and provenance of the new direction

Agreed with the user on 22 October 2026, after two chapters of the earlier course.

| Decision | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Use the main practical part of Lambdin's Gothic book as a structural model | Organize grammatical systems into coherent units; historical grammar is supplementary |
| Present full paradigms and whole declensions together | Teach complete cases/numbers and characteristic changes when a class is introduced |
| Keep Cook's exercises and vocabulary | Regroup them under the grammar sequence while preserving original identifiers and prompts |
| Multiple Cook exercises may form one chapter | The earlier one-to-one rule is superseded |
| A large vocabulary is acceptable | The unit's requirements determine the list; thirty words is no longer a planning target |
| Make readings more substantial | Seek connected passages and provide the verbal/syntactic groundwork as well as vocabulary |
| Start a fresh repository | Preserve prior evidence; reset every teaching milestone and chapter assignment |
| Use Beads if useful for multiple agents | Seed dependencies and ownership; keep source verification and learning coverage in the editorial records |

The audience, terse style, evidence-based corrections, section endnotes, independent review, and full LuaLaTeX exports carry forward. No prior Old English is assumed. Standard grammar terminology is assumed familiar.

The opening noun unit combines Exercises 1–3 and introduces the relevant full declensions, article, basic case uses, stem changes, and the present/preterite indicative copula. Independent source and curriculum work settled the [eighteen-unit outline](../curriculum/COURSE_PLAN.md) and [complete Cook allocation](../curriculum/COOK_MAP.md); the [Unit 1 contract](../chapters/ch01/coverage.json) was saved before prose at `09990cf`. Exercise 4.1–17 belongs to adjective work in Unit 3; its passive command, item 18, is explicitly deferred to Unit 7. Later allocations remain subject to full item-level audits before their contracts.

Sources consulted for the structural comparison: [Lambdin's contents, reproduced by the book distributor](https://cincinnatistate.ecampus.com/introduction-gothic-language-lambdin/bk/9781597523943) and [the publisher's description and preface excerpt](https://wipfandstock.com/9781597523943/an-introduction-to-the-gothic-language/). The full book was not available for inspection. Use this as an organizational comparison; do not claim a page-by-page adaptation or use Gothic paradigms as Old English evidence.

The earlier course is archived with exact Git provenance in `reference/previous-course/PROVENANCE.json`. New-course instructions are at repository root. Historical policies in the archive describe the earlier experiment.
