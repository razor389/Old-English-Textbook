## Proposed criterion for later chapter reviews

1. Preserve every source prompt, source ID, complete paradigm and necessary lexical diagnostic. Novelty is not created by hiding reference information.
2. A transfer item must require at least one grammatical decision from the intended meaning or a changed context: case/role, number, person, tense, agreement controller, head–dependent relation, applicable stem pattern, or an exception. Merely naming a table cell is retrieval, not transfer.
3. A lexical substitution counts as useful transfer when it changes a relevant grammatical choice or tests a different applicable pattern; swapping same-gender/same-pattern/same-number nouns in an unchanged answer is weak evidence.
4. For each newly productive construction or major inflectional contrast, provide at least two independent applications, including one contrast/error or context change that requires choosing the rule. A larger task may cover several features; do not multiply drills mechanically.
5. Do not print a complete target answer or its requested analysis in exposition, worked models, or another new reading. Compare whole responses and semantic role structures, not merely repeated words or unavoidable reference forms.
6. Reference checks and retrieval can remain explicitly labelled, but do not count them toward the two-application threshold. Preserved Cook items may likewise be controlled practice rather than the only transfer evidence.
7. For every task, distinguish what the learner must derive from what the help already states. Guided comprehension of supplied material is legitimate, but cannot certify its independent recall or a new productive milestone.
8. Do not create difficulty by removing prerequisites, inventing unattested forms/senses, multiplying ambiguities without meaning cues, requiring untaught pronouns, or silently expanding reference-only cells into production.
9. Require at least one fresh connected reading and one cumulative task per unit that combine taught decisions, with explicit support for every remaining dependency. The full key stays separate.

For each task record **target feature → prior model/reference → new grammatical decision → status and exact support → exposure check**. This creates a concrete reviewable test. The two-application threshold concerns each major newly productive construction or contrast, not every synonym or every table cell. A single integrated task can cover several targets.
