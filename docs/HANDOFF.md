1. Read root `AGENTS.md`, `STATUS.md`, `EDITORIAL_POLICY.md`, and `docs/WORKFLOW.md`.
2. Run `bd prime`, `bd ready`, and `bd show <id>` for the chosen issue. Use `docs/SEED_TASKS.md` as an initial index.
3. Read `curriculum/COURSE_PLAN.md`, `curriculum/COOK_MAP.md`, `curriculum/UNIT_01_BRIEF.md`, and the active registers. Both registers are empty.
4. Start the ready source-mapping, sequence-design, and first-unit source-audit tasks in parallel if agents are available. Keep their writing scopes exclusive. The coordinator alone updates Beads in this local setup.
5. Reconcile the evidence into the plan, then create the first unit's coverage contract before drafting. Follow the dependency graph through authorship, independent solving, typesetting, and final integration.

Reuse the archived source images, verified transcriptions, evidence, and correction C01. Full scans are cached under `sources/cache/`; URLs and page conventions are in the registry. Original scan facts remain useful; old chapter boundaries, assumptions of prior learning, and reader deferrals must be reconsidered.

The first deliverable is a settled course outline and Cook-to-unit mapping, followed by one complete opening unit. Do not draft later chapters in advance.
